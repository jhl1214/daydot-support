A SMALL PLACE FOR DAYS

Small to write,
long to remember.

Daydot keeps three gentle tools close: a photograph, a note, and the choice of when a day should return.

01

Photo + one line

Keep a title, memo, date, and the photo you choose as one small entry.

02

Days that return

Set an optional local reminder for an annual day you want to remember.

03

Your photo book

Browse by year and bookmark, then share only the photo card you choose.

PRIVACY, QUIETLY

Close to your day,
clear about your choices.

데이닷은 앱 계정이나 서버 일기 백업·동기화를 제공하지 않습니다. 기록은 기기 안의 JSON 데이터와 사진으로 저장됩니다.Daydot has no app account and no server journal backup or sync. Entries live on your device as JSON data and photos.

사진 선택은 시스템 PhotosPicker를 사용하며, 기록 내용과 사진을 자동으로 업로드하지 않습니다. 공유는 사용자가 고른 카드에 한해 선택한 서비스로 내보내는 동작입니다.Photo selection uses the system PhotosPicker, and journal content is never uploaded automatically. Sharing exports only the card you choose to the service you select.
